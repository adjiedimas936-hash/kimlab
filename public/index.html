FAQ

Questions, answered.

What do you need from me to start?

Your product photos or packaging design, logo and brand colours, a few references for the look you want, where the images will be used, and the deadline.

Will the images look AI-generated?

The goal is a natural, believable photo. Your design is placed and refined by hand so the label, shape and colour stay accurate.

Can you make mockups before we print?

Yes. Send the design file and I can show it on a bottle, box, bag, shirt or screen so you can check it before production.

Which files will I get?

High-resolution JPG or PNG in the sizes you need, plus layered PSD files when you want to keep editing.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the number of images and the type of work. You get a confirmed timeline after I see the brief.

Have a product to show?

Send a few details about your product and where the images will be used. I will reply with ideas and a timeline.
